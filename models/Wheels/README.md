## Wheels
The wheels consist of custom-designed PLA rims with TPA95 filament tyres.

This approach allows the wheel dimensions, tyre profile and hub geometry to be modified specifically for the chassis rather than relying on commercially available wheels.

The compliant TPA95 tyre provides greater grip than a rigid printed wheel and helps absorb small irregularities in the track. The PLA rim provides a lightweight and rigid structure for mounting the tyre and connecting it to the axle.

The wheel diameter is also an important parameter because it directly affects vehicle speed: v=πDN/60 where D is wheel diameter and N is motor RPM.

Therefore, the wheel dimensions were selected together with the 300 RPM N20 motor rather than independently.
