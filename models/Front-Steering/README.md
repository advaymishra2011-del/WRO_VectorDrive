## Front - Steering
The front wheels use a parallel-Ackermann-style steering mechanism controlled by an MG90S servo. The steering geometry was chosen to provide controlled directional changes while keeping the steering mechanism within the limited width of the chassis.

The use of a mechanical steering linkage allows both front wheels to turn together while maintaining a consistent relationship between their steering angles. This is preferable to independently controlling two steering motors because it reduces the number of actuators, control complexity and occupied space.

The steering servo is positioned so that its movement can be transferred directly to the steering linkage without requiring a large mechanism or additional motor.
