## Rear Wheel Drive
The rear wheels are driven by a single N20 geared motor through a simple spur-gear transmission. A single motor was selected instead of independently driving the two rear wheels because the differential requirement is relatively small for the compact vehicle and the simpler drivetrain reduces:
- Weight,
- Electrical Complexity
- Number of motors
- Number of motor drivers
- Mechanical failure points.

The spur gear transfers the motor's rotation to a common rear axle, allowing both rear wheels to receive drive from the same motor.

The gear ratio can also be adjusted during testing to trade maximum speed for torque and acceleration.
